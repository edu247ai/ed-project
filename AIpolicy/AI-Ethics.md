# EDU247 AI Ethics

## Purpose

This policy describes the ethical principles expected of EDU247 AI features.
It complements the [AI Policy](./AI-Policy.md) and does not replace legal,
privacy, security, accessibility, or safeguarding review.

## Principles

### Human agency and dignity

AI should support human learning and teaching. Users should retain meaningful
choice, be able to question or correct AI output, and have access to a human
when the decision or impact warrants it. AI must not be used to manipulate,
shame, or coerce learners.

### Fairness and inclusion

Evaluate systems for unequal performance or impact across relevant user
groups, languages, accessibility needs, and educational contexts. Use
appropriate, lawful evaluation data and document limitations. Do not claim a
system is unbiased; record what was tested, what was not tested, and known
risks.

### Educational benefit

Use AI for a clear educational or operational purpose. Generated explanations,
recommendations, and learning materials should be age-appropriate, respectful,
and suitable for educator review where needed. AI must not be treated as a
substitute for qualified educators or trusted support.

### Privacy and respect

Minimize personal data and use it only for a documented purpose. Avoid
unnecessary inferences about a learner's identity, ability, behavior, or
wellbeing. Protect confidential information from unauthorized model providers,
training use, logging, and retention.

### Accountability

A named product or service owner must remain responsible for the design,
deployment, monitoring, and consequences of an AI feature. A model's output is
not a justification for an otherwise unfair or unsupported action.

## Ethical review questions

Before release, ask:

- Who benefits, who could be harmed, and who may be excluded?
- Is AI necessary for this purpose, and is there a less intrusive alternative?
- Can users understand, challenge, or decline the AI-supported action?
- Are quality and fairness evaluations relevant to the actual users and use?
- Could the output create stigma, over-reliance, or a misleading impression of
  certainty?
- Are data collection, retention, and provider sharing proportionate?

Document answers and mitigations in the feature's review record. Unresolved
material harm or unfair impact blocks release.

## Reporting concerns

Users and staff should have a clear way to report harmful, misleading, or
unfair AI behavior. The service owner should assess the impact, take protective
action where needed, and document resolution under the [AI Safety
process](./AI-Safety.md).
